HLA Compatibility Matcher

Local allele catalog lookup, comparison of typed allele strings, and CSV processing.

Research demonstration only. The lookup uses eight hard-coded HLA entries, not IMGT/HLA or an eplet database. Allele-string overlap is not a virtual crossmatch, donor compatibility score, mismatch grade, or transplant risk estimate. Do not use for allocation or clinical decisions. Use only de-identified test data.
